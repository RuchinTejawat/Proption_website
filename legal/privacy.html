Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Proption Fintech collects, uses and protects your personal information, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian laws.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, phone number, email, city, and details you share in forms, enrolments or when booking a call.
  • KYC information required by SEBI for research clients, such as PAN and address proof, collected only when you sign up for research services.
  • Payment information: processed by our payment provider. We do not store your card or bank account numbers.
  • Usage data: basic, anonymous information such as pages visited and device type, used to keep the website working well.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the courses and services you sign up for, and to contact you about them.
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations, including SEBI record-keeping requirements.
  • To respond to your questions and complaints.
  • To send updates you have agreed to receive. You can opt out at any time.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us operate (for example payment, email and hosting providers) under confidentiality obligations, or with regulators and authorities when required by law.

4. Security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your data. We keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law (SEBI requires certain records to be kept for at least five years).

5. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, by writing to proptionfintech@gmail.com. Some data may need to be retained to meet legal obligations.

6. Cookies

This website does not use advertising cookies. Your browser may store small preferences locally to make the site work.
