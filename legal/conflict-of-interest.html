Conflict of Interest Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how Proption Fintech and CA Prachi Mehta, SEBI Registered Research Analyst (Reg. No. INH000017879), identify, avoid and disclose conflicts of interest.

Our principles

  • Research is prepared independently and is not influenced by anyone who could benefit from a particular view.
  • Investor interests come before the interests of the Research Analyst, the firm or any associate.
  • Where a conflict exists, it is disclosed clearly in the research or on this website.

Personal trading

  • The Research Analyst and associates do not trade in a security against their own recommendation.
  • Personal trading restrictions around the publication of research are followed as required by SEBI regulations.
  • Members of the team trade the same quantitative strategies described on this website in their own accounts. This is disclosed so that investors understand the team's own capital is exposed to the same risks.

Compensation

  • No compensation is received from any company whose securities are covered in our research.
  • The Research Analyst's remuneration is not linked to any specific recommendation or to brokerage generated.
  • Where we receive referral fees from brokers for account opening, this is disclosed at the time of referral and does not affect our research.

Separation of activities

Education (Proption Academy) and research services are kept separate. Educational content is not a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

Disclosures in research

Each research report states whether the Research Analyst or associates hold a financial interest or position in the subject security, and any other material conflict.
