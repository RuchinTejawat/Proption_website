We take every complaint seriously. Here is how to reach us and how complaints are handled.

Step 1: Contact us

Write to proptionfintech@gmail.com or call +91 93214 74231 (Monday–Friday, 10 AM–6 PM IST). Include your name, registered phone or email, and details of the issue. We acknowledge complaints within 2 working days and aim to resolve them within 21 calendar days.

Step 3: SEBI SCORES

If your complaint is not resolved to your satisfaction, or not resolved within 21 days, you may lodge it with SEBI on the SCORES portal at scores.sebi.gov.in, or through the SCORES mobile app.

Step 4: Online Dispute Resolution (ODR)

If the complaint is still unresolved after SCORES, you may initiate dispute resolution through the Online Dispute Resolution portal at smartodr.in.

Our monthly complaint data is published on the Complaints Status page.
