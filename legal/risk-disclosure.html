Standard Risk Disclosure

Last updated: November 2026

Investments in securities markets are subject to market risks. Read all related documents carefully before investing.

Risk disclosure on derivatives

  • 9 out of 10 individual traders in the equity Futures & Options segment incurred net losses, according to SEBI studies.
  • On average, loss-makers registered net trading losses close to ₹50,000.
  • Over and above the net trading losses, loss-makers spent an additional 28% of net trading losses as transaction costs.
  • Those making net trading profits incurred between 15% and 50% of such profits as transaction costs.

Source: SEBI study on F&O trading by individual traders.

General risks

  • Market risk: prices can move against a position quickly, and losses can exceed the premium received when selling options.
  • Leverage: derivatives are leveraged. A small market move can cause a large gain or loss relative to the capital used.
  • Liquidity and slippage: orders may fill at worse prices than expected, especially in fast markets or far-from-the-money strikes.
  • Volatility and gap risk: events, news and expiry-day moves can cause sharp, sudden price changes.
  • Technology risk: automated or algorithmic execution can fail due to broker API, internet, power or software issues.
  • Regulatory risk: changes in exchange or SEBI rules (lot sizes, margins, expiries) can affect strategy performance.
