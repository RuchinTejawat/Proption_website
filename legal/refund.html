Refund Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Proption Academy (courses)

  • You can request a full refund up to before the cohort start date.
  • After the cohort has started, or once you have accessed recorded content, course fees are non-refundable. You may instead request a transfer to a later cohort, once, at no extra cost.
  • If we cancel or postpone a cohort and you cannot join the new dates, you receive a full refund.

Research services (SEBI Registered Research Analyst)

  • Fees are charged only as per the agreed terms and SEBI's fee rules for Research Analysts.
  • If you stop the service before the end of the paid period, the fee for the unused period is refunded on a pro-rata basis, without any breakage fee, as required by SEBI.
  • No refund is linked to trading profits or losses. No returns are guaranteed.

How to request a refund

Email proptionfintech@gmail.com with your name, registered phone number and payment reference. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method.
