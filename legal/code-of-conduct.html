Code of Conduct

Last updated: November 2026

CA Prachi Mehta, SEBI Registered Research Analyst (Reg. No. INH000017879), and everyone associated with research at Proption Fintech follow the Code of Conduct for Research Analysts under the SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014.

1. Honesty and good faith

We act honestly and in good faith, and put the interests of investors first.

2. Diligence

We act with due skill, care and diligence. Every recommendation has a reasonable basis supported by research, data and analysis.

3. Conflict of interest

We avoid conflicts of interest wherever possible. Where a conflict cannot be avoided, we disclose it clearly. See our Conflict of Interest Policy.

4. Insider trading and front running

We do not trade on unpublished price-sensitive information, and we do not front-run our own research or recommendations.

5. Confidentiality

We keep client information confidential and use it only to provide our services, unless disclosure is required by law.

6. Professional standards

We maintain high professional standards, keep our knowledge current, and do not make exaggerated, misleading or guaranteed-return claims.

7. Compliance

We comply with all applicable SEBI regulations, circulars and guidelines, maintain required records, and cooperate fully with regulators.

8. Responsibility of senior management

Senior management is responsible for making sure this Code is followed and for putting the right systems in place to monitor compliance.
