Investor Charter

Last updated: November 2026

This Investor Charter is published in line with SEBI's Investor Charter for Research Analysts (revised June 2025). It explains what you can expect from us, how to raise a grievance, and your rights and responsibilities as an investor. Research on this platform is provided by CA Prachi Mehta, SEBI Registered Research Analyst (Reg. No. INH000017879).

Vision

To provide honest, independent and transparent research that helps investors make informed decisions.

Mission

  • Publish research that is unbiased, well-reasoned and free from conflicts of interest.
  • Disclose all material information, including risks and any conflicts, clearly and on time.
  • Keep the investor's interest first and maintain the highest standards of integrity and confidentiality.

Services we provide

  • Research reports and recommendations on securities and derivatives, prepared using quantitative and statistical methods.
  • Explanations of the rationale, risks and assumptions behind each piece of research.
  • Timely updates when a view changes.
  • Investor education (separate from research services).

Grievance redressal

  1. Write to us: proptionfintech@gmail.com or call +91 93214 74231. We acknowledge every complaint and aim to resolve it within 21 calendar days.
  2. SEBI SCORES: if you are not satisfied, or we do not respond in time, lodge a complaint at scores.sebi.gov.in.
  3. Online Dispute Resolution: if still unresolved, use the ODR portal at smartodr.in.

See Grievance Redressal for the full process and Complaints Status for our monthly complaint data.

Your rights as an investor

  • Receive research that is fair, independent and free from misleading claims.
  • Know the Research Analyst's registration details, terms of service and fees before you pay.
  • Receive full disclosure of any conflict of interest.
  • Have your personal information kept confidential.
  • Be heard, and have complaints resolved promptly and fairly.
  • Stop the service at any time, with fee refunded as per SEBI rules (see Refund Policy).

What we expect from investors (Dos & Don'ts)

Do

  • Check the Research Analyst's registration on the SEBI website before taking any service.
  • Pay fees only through banking channels, and keep receipts.
  • Read the terms, disclosures and risk statements carefully.
  • Take your own decision based on your risk appetite and financial situation.

Don't

  • Don't share your trading account credentials, OTPs or passwords with anyone, including us.
  • Don't trust any promise of assured or guaranteed returns. No registered Research Analyst can guarantee returns.
  • Don't make payments in cash or to anyone's personal account other than the one we specify on the invoice.
  • Don't follow tips from unregistered persons on social media or messaging apps.
